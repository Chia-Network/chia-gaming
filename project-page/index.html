Real Gaming.No Casino.

Play directly with another person. Chia Gaming brings two-player games to Chia state channels, with blockchain-enforced rules and no casino holding the pot.

The players play.
The chain keeps score.

Both players fund a shared channel on the Chia blockchain. Moves travel directly between the players through a hub, without waiting for a block every turn. Signed game states let either player take the game on-chain when needed.

01 / CONNECT

Find your opponent

Connect a wallet, join a hub, and agree on the funds each player will contribute.

02 / PLAY

Keep the game moving

Exchange signed moves off-chain. The hub relays messages; it doesn’t hold the players’ funds.

03 / SETTLE

Let the rules decide

Close the channel together, or use on-chain rules and timeouts to finish without the other player’s cooperation.
